# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook

Version 4.2. This handbook applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Robotics Ltd.
It is maintained by the People Operations team. Questions about this handbook should be
sent to people-ops@northwind.example.

## 1. Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours,
employees may arrange their schedule flexibly with their manager, provided they work
37.5 hours per week on average over a four-week period.

Overtime must be approved in advance by a manager. Approved overtime is compensated
with time off in lieu at a rate of 1.5 hours for every hour worked; it is not paid out
in cash except for hourly-paid warehouse staff.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition
to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their
contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
must be used by 31 March; any carried-over days not used by then are forfeited.

Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least four
weeks in advance through the HR portal. Shorter requests need one week's notice.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees who are unwell should notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of
absence. For absences longer than 3 consecutive calendar days, a doctor's note is
required. Northwind pays full salary for up to 20 sick days per year; beyond that,
statutory sick pay applies.

## 4. Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents,
including adoptive parents, are entitled to 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which
can be taken at any time within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote work

Employees in office-based roles may work remotely for up to 3 days per week, subject to
team agreements. Warehouse and field-service roles are not eligible for regular remote
work because they require on-site presence.

### 5.1 Working from another country

Working temporarily from a country other than your country of employment is permitted
for up to 20 working days per calendar year. It requires written approval from both
your manager and the People Operations team at least two weeks before travel, because
of tax and immigration implications. Working from a sanctioned country is never
permitted.

## 6. Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are necessary, reasonable and approved.
Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.

- Meals while travelling: up to 60 EUR per day, including tips.
- Hotels: up to 180 EUR per night in capital cities and 130 EUR per night elsewhere.
- Receipts are required for every expense above 25 EUR.
- Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for
  longer flights.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client entertainment events approved by a
  director.

## 7. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, conferences and
books related to their role. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
Requests above 500 EUR need manager approval.

## 8. Equipment

New employees receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Employees working remotely may
claim a one-off home-office allowance of 400 EUR for a desk or chair. Company equipment
must be returned within 10 working days of the last day of employment.

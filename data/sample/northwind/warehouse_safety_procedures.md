# Northwind Warehouse Safety Procedures

These procedures apply to all people working in or visiting a Northwind-operated
warehouse, including staff, contractors and customer visitors. Site managers are
responsible for making sure everyone on site has read them.

## Personal protective equipment

High-visibility vests and safety shoes with toe caps are mandatory on the warehouse
floor at all times. Hard hats are required in the racking zones where goods are stored
above 2 metres. Hearing protection must be worn in the packaging area when the
shrink-wrap machines are running.

## Visitors

Visitors must sign in at reception, receive a safety briefing and be accompanied by a
Northwind employee at all times. Visitors may not enter zones where autonomous robots
operate unless the zone has been switched to manual mode by the shift supervisor.

## Walking routes and robot zones

Pedestrians must use the walkways marked in green. Robot operating zones are marked in
yellow floor tape. Pedestrians may cross a robot zone only at the marked crossing points,
where robots automatically slow down. Never step in front of a moving robot to stop it;
use the emergency stop or ask a trained operator.

## Manual handling

Loads heavier than 23 kg must not be lifted by one person. Use a pallet truck or ask a
colleague. Forklifts may only be driven by staff holding a valid Northwind forklift
licence, which must be renewed every three years.

## Fire safety

Fire drills take place twice a year. Fire exits and extinguishers must never be blocked
by pallets. In the event of a fire alarm, leave the building by the nearest exit, go to
the assembly point in the car park and report to your fire warden. Robots stop
automatically when the fire alarm sounds.

## First aid and accidents

Every shift must have at least one trained first aider on site. All accidents and near
misses must be recorded in the incident log before the end of the shift, even if nobody
was hurt. Serious injuries must be reported to the site manager immediately.

## Battery charging areas

Robot and forklift batteries may only be charged in the designated charging areas,
which must be kept clear of combustible materials. Smoking and open flames are
prohibited within 10 metres of a charging area. If a battery is swollen, hot to the
touch or smoking, evacuate the area and call the site manager; do not move the battery.

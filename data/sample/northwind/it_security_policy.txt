NORTHWIND ROBOTICS - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This policy sets the minimum security requirements for everyone who uses Northwind
systems, including employees, contractors and temporary staff. Violations may lead to
disciplinary action.

2. PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

Passwords for Northwind accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made
of several unrelated words are encouraged. Passwords must not be reused across
services and must be stored only in the company password manager (Bitwarden).

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the source code
repositories and all cloud consoles. Hardware security keys are required for
administrators of production systems. SMS codes are not an accepted second factor.

Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. They must be changed immediately if
there is any suspicion that they have been exposed.

3. DEVICES

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on
macOS) and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system
security updates must be installed within 14 days of release. Critical updates flagged
by the security team must be installed within 48 hours.

Personal devices may access email and chat through the managed mobile app only. They
may not be used to access source code, customer data or production systems.

4. REMOTE ACCESS

Access to internal systems from outside the office requires the corporate VPN. Public
Wi-Fi may be used only while connected to the VPN.

5. DATA HANDLING

Customer data must never be copied to personal storage, personal email or unapproved
cloud services. Data classified as Confidential must be encrypted when sent outside
the company. Printed confidential documents must be shredded, not placed in regular
recycling.

6. REPORTING SECURITY INCIDENTS

Any suspected security incident - for example a lost laptop, a phishing email that was
clicked, or unexpected account activity - must be reported to the security team within
1 hour of discovery, by email to security@northwind.example or by calling the 24/7
security hotline on extension 4400. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself.

Lost or stolen devices will be remotely wiped. Reporting quickly is never penalised;
failing to report is.

7. ACCESS REVIEWS

Managers must review their team's access rights every quarter. Access for leavers is
revoked on their last working day by the IT service desk.

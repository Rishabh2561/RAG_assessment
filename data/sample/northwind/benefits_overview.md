# Northwind Robotics — Benefits Overview 2026

This overview summarises the benefits available to permanent employees. It does not
replace your employment contract; where the two differ, your contract applies.

## Health insurance

Northwind provides private health insurance for every permanent employee from their
first day of employment. Partners and children can be added to the policy; the company
pays 50% of the premium for family members. Dental cover is included; optical cover is
limited to one eye test and 150 EUR towards glasses every two years.

## Wellbeing

Employees can access a confidential employee assistance programme 24 hours a day,
offering counselling, legal and financial advice. The company reimburses gym or sports
club memberships up to 30 EUR per month.

## Travel to work

The cycle-to-work scheme lets employees lease a bicycle, including e-bikes up to
3,000 EUR, through salary sacrifice over 36 months. Employees who commute by public
transport receive a monthly travel allowance of 50 EUR.

## Life insurance

Life insurance cover of four times the annual base salary is provided to all permanent
employees at no cost. Employees should nominate beneficiaries through the HR portal.

## Recognition

Employees reaching 5 and 10 years of service receive an additional 2 days of annual
leave in that year, and a gift voucher of 250 EUR and 500 EUR respectively.

## Employee referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and passes probation receive a referral
bonus of 1,000 EUR. Referrals of hiring managers or of former employees who left in the
last 12 months are not eligible.

# Riverside Community Garden — Plot Holder Rules

Welcome to Riverside Community Garden. These rules were agreed at the 2026 annual
general meeting and apply to every plot holder.

## Plots and fees

Plots are allocated for one growing season, from 1 March to 30 November. The annual fee
is 45 EUR for a full plot and 25 EUR for a half plot. Members over 65 and students pay
half price. Fees are due by 15 February; unpaid plots are offered to the waiting list.

## Keeping your plot

At least 75% of each plot must be under cultivation by 1 June. A plot that is left
uncultivated for six consecutive weeks during the season may be reallocated after one
written warning.

Paths between plots must be kept clear and no wider than 60 cm of your own border may be
used for storage.

## Water

Hoses may only be used before 09:00 and after 18:00. Watering cans can be used at any
time. During a drought notice from the city, hoses are banned completely.

## Chemicals

Only organic fertilisers and pest controls are allowed. Slug pellets containing
metaldehyde are strictly prohibited because they harm hedgehogs and birds.

## Tools and shed

The shared tool shed code is changed every season and is sent to members by email. Tools
must be cleaned and returned the same day. The shed is locked at sunset.
